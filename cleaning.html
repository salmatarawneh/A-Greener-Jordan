Volunteer action

Small teams can make a visible difference.

Keep the work safe, simple and respectful of the place.

01

Choose

Pick a safe area and confirm permission.

02

Prepare

Bring gloves, bags, water, visible clothing and basic first aid.

03

Clean

Collect litter away from traffic and unsafe areas.

04

Finish

Arrange proper disposal. Never leave collected waste beside the road.

Leave every place better than you found it.

Cleaner roads and public spaces make the country feel cared for — and they make future planting projects easier to protect.

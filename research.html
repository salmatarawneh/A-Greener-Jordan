Planting

How to give a young tree a chance.

Young trees need care. The goal is to help roots establish so extra irrigation can be reduced over time.

01

Choose the place

Check ownership, safety, soil, sunlight, drainage, elevation and local climate.

02

Plant in season

Where suitable, plant during the cool or rainy season so rainfall can help with establishment.

03

Catch rain

Use suitable basins, contours or micro-catchments to keep rain near roots.

04

Protect & check

Protect from grazing and damage. Check survival at 3, 6, 12 and 24 months.

Water matters

Jordan faces severe water scarcity. There is no single correct litres-per-tree number for every site. Watering depends on tree size, soil, heat, wind and rainfall.

General guidance uses trunk size as one starting point for new trees, then reduces watering as roots establish. In Jordan, rainwater capture and good site choice are especially important.

What we measure

  • Survival after 12 and 24 months
  • Water used per surviving tree
  • Native or locally suitable species
  • Land restored and volunteer hours

Our Idea

Green Jordan, together.

Make it easier for people to find good places to help nature, plant suitable trees, clean shared spaces and care for them afterwards.

What we care about

  • Healthy trees that fit local conditions.
  • Less waste on roads, in parks and in public places.
  • Water-smart restoration, especially in dry areas.
  • Neighbours, schools and local groups working together.
